Sam Altman

Sam Altman, tech entrepreneur and CEO of OpenAI, known for his leadership in artificial intelligence innovation.

Sam Altman is an American entrepreneur, investor, and programmer, best known as the CEO of OpenAI. Previously, he was the president of the startup accelerator Y Combinator, where he helped nurture and fund many successful tech companies.

As CEO of OpenAI, Altman has played a major role in advancing artificial intelligence and promoting responsible AI development. Under his leadership, OpenAI released several groundbreaking models including GPT-4 and ChatGPT, shaping the conversation around AI's potential and ethics.

Altman is also recognized for his visionary insights into the future of technology and humanity, often speaking publicly about the societal impacts of AI, energy, and global policy.
